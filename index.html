Space Case: In Space Case by
Stuart Gibbs, twelve-year-old Dashiell "Dash" Gibson is bored living on Moon Base Alpha until the base's top scientist, Dr. Holtz, turns up dead.While base commander Nina claims the death
is an accidental suicide from an unsealed spacesuit, Dash suspects foul play because he overheard Dr. Holtz excitedly talking about a major upcoming discovery. With help from a newcomer named Kira, Dash uncovers a hidden sign-language message from the victim that
exposes a maintenance worker named Garth as the killer. Garth threatened the base to keep Dr. Holtz's monumental discovery a secret: intelligent alien life. In the end, Dash meets Zan, the actual alien presence monitoring humanity, and agrees to become her secret earthly contact.
